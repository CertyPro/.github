# Code of conduct

This code of conduct applies across the **CertyPro** organisation. Many of our
repositories are learning spaces, so being welcoming matters here.

## Our pledge

We want a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of experience,
background, or identity. First-time contributors are especially welcome.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind, patient, and constructive.
- Help newcomers and assume good intent.
- Accept feedback gracefully and give it respectfully.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove or reject contributions that breach this code, and may
block repeat or serious offenders. Report concerns privately via a repository's
**Security** tab advisory or to **conduct@certy.pro**.
